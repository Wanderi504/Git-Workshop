Simple Pilau Recipe

Ingredients:
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tbsp pilau masala
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
- 1 carrot, diced (optional)
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped (optional)
- 1 small piece ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Fresh coriander, chopped (optional)

Instructions:
1. Wash the rice well and soak it for 15–20 minutes. Drain.
2. Heat oil or ghee in a pot and fry the onion until golden.
3. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin seeds, and pilau masala. Stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add the tomatoes, salt, black pepper, and turmeric. Cook for 2 minutes.
5. Add the stock and bring to a boil.
6. Stir in the rice, carrot, and bell pepper if using.
7. Cover and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes until the rice is soft and fluffy.
8. Let it sit for 5 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork.
9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve warm.

Serves: 3–4
